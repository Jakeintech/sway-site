How it plays

Take the safe night, or push it.

Perch
Drag a bird onto a branch. A ghost shows where it will land and how far the branch will lean before you commit.
Slip
Past 24 degrees birds start to slip. A lower branch catches them if you left room; otherwise they fly off into the dark.
Goodnight
Once the night's quota is home, Goodnight banks it. Every bird past the quota is worth more than the last.
Seven nights
A run is seven nights and three hearts. A night with nobody lost gives a heart back. Wind comes after every third bird.
Twelve birds
The Dove calms its neighbours. The Pigeon never settles. The Puffin grips hardest. The Hummingbird weighs almost nothing.
The Daily
The same birds and the same branch for every player, once a day, one try.
