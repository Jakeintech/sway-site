How it plays

Far out scores more. Far out tips more.

  • Hang

    Take a piece from the tray and hang it on a rod. The farther from the pivot, the more Points it scores and the more it tips. A dashed outline shows where the mobile will settle before you let go.

  • Merge

    Matching pieces that touch merge into the next piece up the ladder. The ladder ends at the Brass Sun.

  • Hold on

    Gusts swing the whole mobile. A rod tilted past 25° sheds its pieces. A lower rod can catch them.

A run

8 commissions. About 12 to 15 minutes.

A commission is one mobile. It gives you a score target and a fixed number of pieces to reach it with.

Score
Your score is Points × Mult. Points come from where you hang each piece.
The studio
Between commissions you visit the studio for Charms and Tools.
Charms
Modifiers that bend the rules. You hold up to 5 Charms.
Tools
One-shot items. Use a Tool once and it is gone.
Daily commission
A commission that uses the same seed for everyone. Its leaderboard uses Game Center, which is optional.
Gallery
Your finished mobiles are kept in a gallery. You can share a picture of one if you like.
