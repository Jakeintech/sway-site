Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

The short version.

  • Sway collects no personal data.
  • No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your game progress, settings and gallery are saved on your iPhone only.
  • The game has no server of its own. Game Center is optional and is used only for the daily leaderboard.
  • Sharing a picture of a mobile uses the iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share.

1. Who we are

Sway is a game for iPhone made by Jake Williams ("we", "us"). It is in development and is coming to the App Store. This policy explains what information the game handles, where it goes and the choices you have. It covers test builds as well as the released game. Questions: info@jakeawilliams.com.

3. No server of its own

Sway has no server and no backend, and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software. The only online feature is the daily leaderboard, which Apple's Game Center provides.

4. Tracking and permissions

Sway does not track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt. It does not use your location, camera, microphone or contacts. If you save a shared picture to Photos from the share sheet, iOS may ask whether Sway may add it; Sway does not read your photo library.

5. Game Center

Game Center is optional and is used only for the daily leaderboard. If you sign in, Apple runs Game Center and processes that information under its own privacy policy. A leaderboard shows a score beside a Game Center name, as Apple presents it. Sway does not receive your Apple Account, email address or contacts. Game Center is never required to play, and you control your profile, friends and visibility in Settings > Game Center.

6. Sharing a picture

When you share a picture of a mobile, Sway hands the picture to the iOS share sheet and you choose where it goes. What happens next is up to the app or service you pick, under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share.

7. What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in of our own.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No analytics and no tracking.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Your progress, settings and gallery stay on your iPhone until you delete the game, which removes everything Sway stored. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

To delete it, touch and hold Sway on the Home Screen, tap Remove App, then Delete App. Copies inside your own device backups, in iCloud or on a computer, follow the rules of that backup. Scores recorded in Game Center are managed by Apple.

9. Children

Sway collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Game Center is controlled by Apple and can be restricted by a parent with Screen Time. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. Sway keeps your data on your own iPhone, so you can exercise these rights directly by deleting the game. We do not sell or share personal information. Nothing is tracked, so Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals need nothing further from us: the game and this site behave the same way with or without them. If you have a request we can help with, write to info@jakeawilliams.com.

11. This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking or third-party resources; its fonts, images and video are served from the site itself. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

12. Security

Sway relies on the protections built into iOS: its saves sit inside the app's sandbox, protected by your passcode and the device's encryption.

13. App Store privacy label

Sway is not on the App Store yet. When it is, its privacy label there will be set to match this policy. If you ever find the two disagree, tell us and we will correct it.

14. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. For a significant change we will also say so in the game.
