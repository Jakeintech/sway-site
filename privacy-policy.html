Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

The short version.

  • Sway collects no personal data.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your game progress and settings are saved on your iPhone only.
  • The one optional purchase, The Full Flock, is handled entirely by Apple.
  • Sharing a clip uses the iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share.

1. Who we are

Sway is a game for iPhone made by Jake Williams ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles, where it goes and the choices you have. It covers TestFlight builds as well as the App Store version. Questions: info@jakeawilliams.com.

2. What the game handles

3. No server of its own

Sway has no server and no backend, and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software. It works offline.

4. Tracking and permissions

Sway does not track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo library. If you save a shared clip to Photos from the share sheet, iOS may ask whether Sway may add it.

5. What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in of our own.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • No analytics and no tracking.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your progress and settings stay on your iPhone until you delete the game, which removes everything Sway stored. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Your purchase stays with your Apple Account and can be restored.

7. Children

Sway collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases can be restricted by a parent with Screen Time and Ask to Buy. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. Sway keeps your data on your own iPhone, so you can exercise these rights directly by deleting the game. If you have a request we can help with, write to info@jakeawilliams.com.

9. This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking or third-party resources; its fonts and images are served from the site itself. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

10. App Store privacy label

Sway's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected", matching this policy. If you ever find the two disagree, tell us and we will correct it.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
