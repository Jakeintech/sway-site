Getting Sway

When does Sway come out?

Sway is in development and is coming to the App Store. There is no release date yet.

What will it cost?

One price, paid once. The planned price is USD 3.99. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no account.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Sway plays in portrait, and one hand is enough.

Playing

How does a commission work?

A commission is one round. It gives you a score target and a fixed number of pieces. You hang the pieces on the mobile one at a time and try to reach the target.

How is the score worked out?

Your score is Points × Mult. A piece hung farther from the pivot scores more Points, and it tips the mobile more.

What makes pieces merge?

Matching pieces that touch merge into the next piece up the ladder. The ladder runs Bead, Disc, Half-moon, Triangle, Brass Sun.

What do gusts do?

A gust swings the whole mobile. A rod tilted past 25° sheds its pieces. A lower rod can catch them.

What are Charms and Tools?

Between commissions you visit the studio. Charms are modifiers that bend the rules, and you hold up to 5 of them. Tools are one-shot items.

How long is a run?

A run is 8 commissions and takes about 12 to 15 minutes.

What is the daily commission?

A commission that uses the same seed for everyone. Its leaderboard uses Game Center, which is optional.

Saves

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone only. That covers your progress, your settings and your gallery of finished mobiles. Sway has no account and no server, so there is no copy anywhere else, and deleting the app deletes them.

Do I need Game Center?

No. Game Center is optional and is used only for the daily leaderboard.

Purchase and privacy

Are there in-app purchases or ads?

No. Sway is a premium game: one price, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

What data does Sway collect?

None. Sway has no analytics, no tracking and no account. The privacy policy has the detail.

What happens when I share a picture of a mobile?

Sway hands the picture to the iOS share sheet and you choose where it goes. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the game: touch and hold Sway on the Home Screen, tap Remove App, then Delete App. That removes your progress, settings and gallery. We hold no copy, so there is nothing else to delete. The privacy policy has more.
