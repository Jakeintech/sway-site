Playing

How do I perch a bird?

Drag up from anywhere and move the bird over a branch. A ghost shows where it will land and how far the branch will lean. Let go to perch it. A quick tap does nothing, so a stray touch never drops a bird.

Why did my bird slip?

Past 24 degrees of lean, birds start to slip. A slipping bird is caught by a lower branch if one has room; otherwise it flies off and costs a heart.

When should I say Goodnight?

Goodnight lights up once the night's quota is home and banks the night. Every bird past the quota is worth more than the last, so you can push on, but a bird lost costs a heart.

How do hearts work?

A run has three hearts across seven nights. A night where no bird is lost gives a heart back.

What is the Daily?

The same birds and the same branch for every player, once a day, with one try.

Can I play with VoiceOver?

Yes. Each branch offers its spots as actions that say what the tilt would be, so you can choose where to perch by ear. Sway also follows Reduce Motion and Dynamic Type.

The Full Flock

What is free?

All seven nights of Spring, replayable as often as you like, every Daily and the whole Birdbook. No ads and no subscription.

What does The Full Flock add?

Summer, Autumn and Winter. Each is windier, and the last two bring an extra bird every night. It is one payment, and Family Sharing is supported. The price is shown in the App Store in your own currency.

How do I restore it on a new iPhone?

Settings (the gear on the title screen) > Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone only. Sway has no account and no server, so there is no copy anywhere else, and deleting the app deletes your progress.

What happens when I share a clip?

Sway hands the clip to the iOS share sheet and you choose where it goes. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share.
