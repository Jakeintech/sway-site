Short description

Get the birds to bed before the branch tips.

Long description

Birds arrive one at a time and you perch each one on a hanging branch. A ghost shows where it will land and how far the branch will lean. Past 24 degrees they slip; a lower branch can catch them, or they fly off into the dark.

Once the night's quota is home, Goodnight banks it, and every bird past the quota is worth more than the last. A run is seven nights and three hearts. Twelve birds each behave differently, and they fill the Birdbook as you meet them. A Daily gives every player the same birds and the same branch once a day.
