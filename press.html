Descriptions

In one line

Build a hanging mobile. Every piece tips everything above it.

Short

You hang painted pieces on a mobile one at a time. Farther from the pivot scores more Points but tips more. Each commission has a score target and a fixed number of pieces, and your score is Points × Mult.

Longer

Matching pieces that touch merge up a ladder: Bead, Disc, Half-moon, Triangle, Brass Sun. Gusts swing the mobile. A rod tilted past 25° sheds its pieces, and a lower rod can catch them.

Between commissions you visit the studio for Charms, which bend the rules (you hold up to 5), and Tools, which are one-shot items. A run is 8 commissions, about 12 to 15 minutes. A daily commission uses the same seed for everyone, and your finished mobiles are kept in a gallery.

One price. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account.
